    How to read this

    No major-party competition
    The general-election ballot does not have both a Democrat and a Republican for the seat. That includes seats where one candidate runs alone, seats where only minor-party or independent candidates oppose them, and top-two states where both finalists share a party. This matches Ballotpedia's "major party competition" measure.
    No opponent at all
    No more candidates than seats, so the race is settled once filing closes. Write-in candidates are not counted.
    Not yet listed
    The source page has no candidates for the district yet. These seats are left out of every percentage.

    Data comes from the state election office's official candidate list where one can be read automatically (currently Colorado, Delaware, Florida, Minnesota and New Mexico), and otherwise from each chamber's 2026 election page on Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). It refreshes nightly, and each chamber links to its source. Multi-member districts count every seat. Nebraska's legislature is officially nonpartisan; party labels there come from the candidates' known affiliations. Check a district against your state election office before acting on it.
